# Vision

## The Signal — A Living Digital Commerce World

**Not a store. A living organism.**

The Signal is a viral 3D commerce platform that continuously discovers emerging cultural signals and transforms them into interactive digital experiences users want to explore, share, and purchase from.

---

## Core Thesis

The internet doesn't need another catalog. It needs a **living world** that reflects what's emerging right now — a digital organism that breathes with culture, adapts to attention, and converts curiosity into commerce.

---

## Experience

Users don't browse categories. They:

1. **Arrive** — see the current world state (what's hot right now)
2. **Explore** — interact with 3D objects representing trends/products
3. **Discover** — uncover stories, products, experiences
4. **Create** — generate personal artifacts to share
5. **Transact** — purchase, collect, affiliate-link
6. **Return** — the world has changed since last visit

---

## Visual Identity

- **Futuristic** but warm — not cold corporate sci-fi
- **Cinematic** — every interaction feels intentional
- **Playful** — curiosity rewarded with delight
- **Mysterious** — depth invites exploration
- **Shareable** — every screenshot/artifact is marketing

---

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Daily world state changes | ≥1 |
| User interactions per session | ≥5 |
| Share rate | ≥10% |
| Return visits (7-day) | ≥30% |
| Revenue per visitor | Growing weekly |

---

## Principles

1. **Signal-first** — content emerges from real cultural signals, not assumptions
2. **Experience over catalog** — interaction precedes transaction
3. **Viral by design** — sharing is a feature, not a hope
4. **Zero-budget forever** — every decision must be free to operate
5. **Autonomous by default** — human intervention is the exception
6. **Ship fast, measure faster** — MVP in days, not months
